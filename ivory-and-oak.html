Craftsmanship

From standing timber to finished piece, nothing is rushed.

01 · Selection

Timber, chosen by hand

We buy whole logs from FSC-certified mills and let them air-dry for up to two years before a single cut is made.

02 · Joinery

Joints, not fasteners

Mortise and tenon, dovetail, and draw-bore joinery throughout — no particleboard, no visible screws, nothing to come loose.

03 · Finishing

Oiled, not lacquered

A hardwax oil finish that ages honestly and can be refreshed at home, instead of a plastic coating that only ever looks worse.

Materials

Only what the forest can grow back.

Every board we use is FSC-certified and traceable to the forest it came from. For every tree we take, our mill partners plant three. Finishes are plant-based oils and waxes — nothing that off-gasses, nothing you'd need to ventilate a room for.

  • — Solid hardwood only: walnut, white oak, ash, cedar, maple and elm
  • — FSC chain-of-custody certified from forest to workshop
  • — Hardwax oil and beeswax finishes, VOC-free
  • — Solid brass and blackened steel hardware, no plastic fittings

Questions

Things people usually ask before ordering.

How long does an order actually take? +

Most pieces take six to ten weeks from confirmed drawing to delivery. Larger case pieces or busy seasons can run closer to twelve — we'll always give you a real date, not an optimistic one.

Can I customise dimensions or wood? +

Every piece is made to order, so dimensions are adjustable within the limits of the design, and you can choose from any wood in our current stock. Custom species requests are handled case by case.

What does the warranty actually cover? +

25 years against structural joinery failure under normal household use. Finish wear isn't covered, but it's designed to be refreshed at home — we'll show you how at delivery.

Do you deliver outside the UK? +

Yes — we ship across Europe and to select cities in North America via specialist fine-furniture freight. Delivery costs are quoted individually based on the piece and destination.

How do I care for solid wood long-term? +

A yearly hardwax oil top-up keeps the finish healthy — it takes about twenty minutes per piece. We send every client a small care kit and a short guide with their delivery.
